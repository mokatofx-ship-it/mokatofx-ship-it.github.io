Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy explains how "FX Price Alert Pro" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information the App Collects

The developer does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address. The App stores your settings (registered currency pairs, alarm configurations, etc.) only on your own device and does not transmit this data to any server operated by the developer.

2. Exchange Rate Data

To receive real-time exchange rates, the App can connect to a third-party data provider (such as Finnhub) that you configure yourself. Any API key you enter is stored only on your device and never passes through a server operated by the developer. Please refer to the relevant provider's own privacy policy for how they handle data.

3. Advertising

The App may display ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, including the Advertising ID, for ad delivery and personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy for details:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the full version is processed through Google Play's billing system. Payment information is handled by Google Play and is not shared with the developer.

5. Permissions

The App uses permissions for displaying notifications, vibration, and internet access. These are used solely for the App's core features (alarms and rate retrieval) and for no other purpose.

6. Third-Party Sharing

The developer does not sell or share collected information with third parties, except as described above (advertising and payment processing).

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the developer contact information listed on the Google Play store listing.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time without prior notice. The version posted on this page is the effective version.
